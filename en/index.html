Research notes

What we learn when software meets a real business.

We write up engagements the way field researchers do — a question, a method, findings, and what they imply. Every number below comes from a shipped system.

RN–01Manufacturing · Operations

Removing coordination work from a garment factory

Abstract

A large garment manufacturer was spending roughly 200 hours a week on planning and task tracking spread across spreadsheets and chat. We asked how much of that coordination survives when every surface of the business shares a single data model.

Method

One operating system rolled out across five surfaces — the production floor, an employee app, a B2B wholesale portal, a consumer store, and a retrieval-based AI assistant for leadership — replacing the disconnected tools rather than integrating them.

Findings

−90%
planning & tracking hours
5
surfaces, one data model
1
source of truth

Implication

Most coordination work in a traditional factory is not work — it is the cost of disconnected systems. Remove the disconnection, and the hours go with it.

RN–02Service operations · AI

How far can AI take frontline customer support?

Abstract

Support teams answer the same questions across four or five channels, slowly and inconsistently. Running our own platform in production let us measure how much of the incoming stream a multi-model AI layer resolves without a human.

Method

KulpunAi in production: multiple frontier models behind a routing gateway, unified across WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, and web chat, with CRM context and a human handoff that preserves the full conversation.

Findings

Implication

The ceiling on AI support is not model quality — it is context. Given the business's own data and a clean handoff, most volume never needs a human.

RN–03Consumer fintech · Distribution

In emerging-market lending, distribution is the product

Abstract

Microcredit in most emerging markets runs on branches, paperwork, and slow manual decisions. We asked what happens to reach and volume when the entire loan lifecycle — application, scoring, disbursement, repayment — moves onto the phone.

Method

A mobile-first lending app with an automated application and scoring flow, built to national scale from day one, so a borrower goes from install to credit decision without visiting a branch.

Findings

1M+
downloads on Google Play
0
branch visits required
National
operating scale

Implication

Where desktop banking never arrived, the phone is not a channel. It is the branch, the loan officer, and the ledger at once.

How we work

Traditional problems, modern operating systems.

Most businesses do not need more software. They need fewer systems that disagree with each other.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We map how the business actually runs — the spreadsheets, group chats, and workarounds — and find where hours and money quietly leak.

  2. 02

    Digitize

    Paper, memory, and tribal knowledge become structured data on one model, so every team finally sees the same truth.

  3. 03

    Automate

    The repetitive work — planning, dispatch, reporting, follow-ups — moves into the system and stops consuming people.

  4. 04

    Integrate AI

    AI where it measurably pays: answering customers, drafting documents, and letting leadership query the whole company in plain language.
